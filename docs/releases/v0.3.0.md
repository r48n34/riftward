# RIFTWARD 0.3.0

**Drop into the Reach. Build what survives.**

RIFTWARD is a single-player, first-person extraction shooter built in Godot 4.7.2. This is the third public
build: **Sound & Motion**. The frontier has a voice now — 113 procedural sound effects, eight music cues
that follow the run, a real vehicle stable, four authored cut-scenes and a set of curated CC0 art packs for
impacts, particles and UI — and the world itself grew seven new site kits with a full detail pass.

Windows 10/11 (64-bit), DirectX 12 GPU. Free download, GPL-3.0 source.

## Headline features

- **A real sound-and-music system.** 113 procedural SFX ids (weapons, impacts, abilities, UI, ambience,
  vehicles, bosses, storms, extraction), **eight music cues plus silence** — menu, hub, deploy, combat,
  boss, exfil, storm, results — crossfading over 1.6 s, split across Music/SFX/UI/Ambience/Vehicles buses
  with a master limiter. Audio is still synthesised from hand-authored recipes; where a curated recording
  sells the moment better, seven **CC0 Kenney packs** supply it, with the synth as silent fallback.
- **Seven new site kits and a detail pass.** Foundry Hall, Habitation Cluster, Comms Array, Fuel Depot,
  Field Clinic, Research Lab and Water Works join the frontier (20 site types, 24 base points of interest,
  up to 28 on a crowded map). The 13 original kits got interior, roof and service detail, and every site
  now has barrels, crates, pallets, spools and debris scattered around its landing ring.
- **Five rideables.** The **Rift Rig** (two-seat ground car), **Vale Hoverbike**, **Kestrel Wing**
  (airplane), **Void Skiff** (VTOL spaceship) and the **Warden Steed** (horse). Mount with **E**, whistle
  the horse with **T**; weapons lock while riding, the HUD becomes a speed/hull/boost panel, and dungeons
  are on foot. The rig and hoverbike wait at your drop zone; the specialists are parked at the sites that
  suit them.
- **Four authored cut-scenes.** A short drop recap, a 4 s insertion shot as the run goes live, a 6 s exfil
  sequence that plays *before* the run ends (ramp, lift-off, fade) and a 4 s boss confrontation shot once
  per boss. All are skippable with Esc/Space/Enter or the on-screen Skip, capped at 12 s, and reduce motion
  turns them into a single static card.
- **Cargo and proper caches.** Ten new salvage items — rations, flares, plates, stims, ammo, repair kits,
  fuel cells, map intel, relic shards and trophy cores — rolled through three loot tables
  (`poi_common`, `poi_rare`, `vehicle_stash`). Rich sites pay better; vehicle stashes pay differently.
  The Codex gains **Sites** and **Rideables** tabs.
- **Curated CC0 art.** Seven Kenney packs (267 files, 5.96 MiB) give muzzle flashes, impacts, explosions,
  engines, UI feedback and short music jingles real art. All 3D geometry, materials and shaders remain
  procedural, and the game runs with the packs removed.

## Controls added

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Mount / dismount a rideable | `E` |
| Whistle the Warden Steed | `T` |
| Vehicle throttle / steer | `W` `S` / `A` `D` |
| Vehicle boost | `Shift` |
| Vehicle brake / descend | `Ctrl` or `C` |
| Vehicle jump / ascend | `Space` |
| Vehicle horn | `LMB` |
| Skip a cut-scene | `Esc`, `Space` or `Enter` (or the on-screen Skip) |

On foot nothing changed: Ctrl/C crouch, Space jump, Shift sprint. While riding, those verbs become
brake/descend, jump/ascend and boost. Every action is still rebindable in **Settings → Controls**.

## Tests

The headless suite manifest now lists **18 suites**: the previous 14 plus **audio**, **vehicles**,
**cutscenes** and **assets**. **All 18 suites pass** in the final 0.3.0 gate (aggregate exit 0,
2026-10-26). Audio covers the 113-id catalog, buses/limiter, cue switching and the CC0
fallback (280 checks); vehicles cover all five archetypes, mounting, motion, horse gaits, weapon lock and
the HUD (244 checks); cutscenes cover the four ids, beats, skip, hard cap and reduce-motion card (78
checks); assets verify every curated file's size and sha256, the payload cap and the particle fallback
(2,110 checks). Run them all with `tests\run_all.ps1`; per-suite evidence is in
[docs/QA_REPORT.md](../QA_REPORT.md).

## Fixes

- The real-boot inventory smoke no longer races the insertion cut-scene: input waits for any running
  cinematic before the test injects keys.
- Audio teardown now stops every voice and drops stream references, clearing the exit-time
  `AudioStreamWAV` / resource-leak warnings from the UI, audio and assets suites.
- Missing or unimported CC0 audio files fall back to the procedural synth with no error spam; missing
  particle textures fall back to a procedural soft-dot.

## Saves and upgrading from 0.2.0

- Keep `RIFTWARD.exe` and `RIFTWARD.pck` together; replace the old game folder with this download.
- **Save format is unchanged (schema v5)** — 0.3.0 adds catalog content and no new persistent fields, so
  your profile, outpost layout, inventory and records load exactly as they are. A `.bak` copy is still
  kept, and the save folder (`%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\RIFTWARD`) is untouched by the download.
- **Settings are unchanged** — volumes, display, accessibility and bindings are preserved.

## Install (Windows)

1. Download `RIFTWARD-win64.zip` and unzip it anywhere — for example `C:\Games\RIFTWARD`.
2. Keep `RIFTWARD.exe` and `RIFTWARD.pck` in the same folder. Do not run the executable from inside the
   zip viewer.
3. Run `RIFTWARD.exe`. The first launch can take a few seconds while shaders compile.
4. The build is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" on first
   launch: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Requirements: Windows 10/11 64-bit, DirectX 12 capable GPU, 4 GB RAM minimum (8 GB recommended), about
150 MB free disk space, keyboard and mouse (gamepad supported). No installer and no admin rights needed.
Controls and troubleshooting are in the `README.txt` inside the zip and in
[docs/PLAYER_README.txt](../PLAYER_README.txt).

## Known issues

- Single-player and offline only: no co-op, PvP or online play, and no browser version.
- No mid-run save. Quitting during a deployment abandons that run; meta progress is saved at the hub or
  at the end of a run.
- No installer and no auto-updater; to update, replace the game folder (your save folder is untouched).
- Grenade selection is still partial: frag throws in play while shock and veil are authored but not
  selectable; outpost upgrades still use the UI rather than freeform placement.
- Vehicles are a traversal verb, not a combat one: weapons and abilities are locked while riding, and
  aircraft are not shown on the tactical map.
- No formal performance profiling yet: 60 FPS at 1080p is the target, and the display settings include
  render scale, anti-aliasing and a frame-rate cap if you need to trade quality for frame rate.
