# RIFTWARD 0.2.0

**Drop into the Reach. Build what survives.**

RIFTWARD is a single-player, first-person extraction shooter built in Godot 4.7.2. This is the second
public build: the **Frontier Update** plus the **UI/content expansion**. The frontier is now five planets
wide, the weather and day/night cycle change how a drop plays, there is a dungeon under every frontier and
a guided first drop for new Wardens — and the interface was rebuilt around the things it is about.

Windows 10/11 (64-bit), DirectX 12 GPU. Free download, GPL-3.0 source.

## Headline features

- **Five planets, twelve maps.** Meridian-9, Cinder-4, Thalassa-2, Frost-9 and Nyx-12, each with its own
  biomes, palette, sky, weather pool, water/lava rules and under-reach themes. Pick a planet, a map, a
  difficulty and a mode in Quick Deploy, or override the featured map from the Hub.
- **Weather and a day/night cycle.** 18 weather states from clear skies to whiteouts and rift cyclones,
  with fog, particles, lightning and visibility that changes how far enemies can see. A moving sun, four
  sky keyframes, stars and a night headlamp make time of day matter.
- **Water and lava.** Wade, swim and burn: seeded basins with animated surfaces are part of the terrain,
  the map and the hazard system.
- **Under-reach dungeons.** Three themed dungeon kits generate 2–3 hatches per run at vaults, stockades,
  Choir posts, colony ruins and the derelict freighter. Descend, break the guardian, carry the loot back
  up — the storm pauses underground, the run clock does not, and exfil does not reach you there.
- **Field Training.** A five-stage guided first drop (move, shoot, ability, loot, exfil) with a HUD card,
  skip from pause and replay from the main menu. It never counts as a death.
- **Five ways to drop.** Normal, Quick (2x clock), Blackout (night insertion), Gauntlet (early Collapse)
  and Onslaught (elite surge) rewrite the clock, storm, density, rewards and loot.
- **More to fight.** 37 enemy entries now: 20 base archetypes, 11 elites and 6 bosses across the Ashen,
  Bloom and Choir, including new frontier natives, five new elites and three new phased bosses.
- **More to fight with.** 16 weapons (including the Ember Thrower flamer, Quarry HMG and Scatter Arc),
  24 abilities and 24 perks, plus staged contracts and 20 world events with three firing per run.
- **A visual-first interface.** Image-first Loadout, Locker, Skills, Squad and Outpost screens; planet
  portraits and map thumbnails in Quick Deploy; a Codex with Planets, Weather, Operations and Dungeons
  tabs; a clickable 8x8 outpost diorama; item art, Warden and Driftling portraits and 29 new line icons.
- **A new deployment loading screen.** A four-act drop feed — threat signature boss dossier, rift breach,
  drop descent and touchdown — driven by the actual map, planet, mode and boss of your drop, with skip and
  reduced-motion support.
- **Display settings.** Window mode, resolution, V-Sync, render scale, anti-aliasing, frame-rate cap,
  quality preset and interface scale, applied live and remembered.

## Fixes

- Fixed a crash that could occur when firing the first shot of a deployment in the production scene
  graph.
- Fixed grenade/projectile spawn setup so a thrown grenade always appears at the correct origin instead of
  risking a spawn at the world origin.
- Invalid or stale outpost layouts are now rebuilt safely on load without losing module tiers, currency,
  inventory or records.
- The Reach Event banner, weather/clock chip and hazard tint no longer compete with the combat sightline.

## Saves and upgrading from 0.1.0

- Keep `RIFTWARD.exe` and `RIFTWARD.pck` together; replace the old game folder with this download.
- Your profile/save folder (`%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\RIFTWARD`) is untouched by the download and
  upgrades automatically on first launch: save schema v3 -> v5, with the outpost layout rebuilt to the
  authored v2 plan while tiers, currency, inventory and records are preserved. A `.bak` copy is kept.
- Settings are preserved; new display options take their defaults.

## Install (Windows)

1. Download `RIFTWARD-win64.zip` (about 40 MB) and unzip it anywhere — for example `C:\Games\RIFTWARD`.
2. Keep `RIFTWARD.exe` and `RIFTWARD.pck` in the same folder. Do not run the executable from inside the
   zip viewer.
3. Run `RIFTWARD.exe`. The first launch can take a few seconds while shaders compile.
4. The build is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" on first
   launch: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Requirements: Windows 10/11 64-bit, DirectX 12 capable GPU, 4 GB RAM minimum (8 GB recommended), about
150 MB free disk space, keyboard and mouse (gamepad supported). No installer and no admin rights needed.
Controls and troubleshooting are in the `README.txt` inside the zip and in
[docs/PLAYER_README.txt](../PLAYER_README.txt).

## Known issues

- Single-player and offline only: no co-op, PvP or online play, and no browser version.
- No mid-run save. Quitting during a deployment abandons that run; meta progress is saved at the hub or
  at the end of a run.
- No installer and no auto-updater; to update, replace the game folder (your save folder is untouched).
- Grenade selection is partial: frag throws in play while shock and veil are authored but not selectable.
- The outpost upgrades modules through the UI; freeform drag placement is not exposed in this build.
- No formal performance profiling yet: 60 FPS at 1080p is the target, and the display settings include
  render scale, anti-aliasing and a frame-rate cap if you need to trade quality for frame rate.
